1
The problem

AI systems now take real actions. Every change is a gamble.

  • AI systems move money, change accounts, approve requests and write to production systems.
  • Teams change the model, prompt or tools every few weeks, and any change can silently break behaviour that used to work.
  • Today: manual spot checks, home-grown test scripts, and dashboards that score the model's words, not what it did.
  • Nothing stops an untested change reaching production. Teams slow every release down, or ship and hope.
2
The solution

Bring your system. Requal runs, verifies and enforces.

Teams bring their own AI system, models and evaluation logic. Requal provides the infrastructure that turns a proposed change into a release decision they can trust and prove.

Runs in your cloud

A runner in the customer's environment. Raw data stays there by default.

Checks outcomes

What the system did to the tools and data it touched, not just what it said.

Decides honestly

Sound statistics; says "not enough evidence" instead of passing.

Signs and enforces

Signed, re-verifiable verdicts; the release decision is enforced at the deployment gate.

3
How it works

From a proposed change to an enforced release decision.

1

Compare

Old and new versions run side by side on the same cases, under the same declared conditions.

2

Exercise

Sandboxed copies of tools, resettable state, injected failures, and a log of every action.

3

Qualify

The customer's policy and statistical limits decide: qualified, not qualified, or needs more evidence.

4

Authorise

Named approvers authorise the exact version; the deployment gate admits it and blocks anything else.

4
Why now

AI is moving from answering to acting.

  • Agents are being given tools and permissions inside real business systems.
  • New models ship constantly; teams switch models and prompts far more often than they ship traditional software.
  • A wrong action costs more than a wrong answer, and someone has to sign off on every release.
  • Engineering teams need release infrastructure for AI the way they already have CI/CD for code.
